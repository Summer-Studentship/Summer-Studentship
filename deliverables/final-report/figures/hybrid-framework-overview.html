Source and terrain

Regional2D propagation

  • Conserved state [h, qₓ, qᵧ]

03

One-way scale transfer

  • Selected wet nearshore interface
  • η(t), h(t) and u(t) forcing histories
  • Regional 245-545 s mapped to local 0-300 s
04

Local3D replay

  • Incompressible two-phase URANS-VOF
  • k-ω SST turbulence baseline
  • No-defence and rigid simple-barrier cases
05

Evidence outputs

  • Velocity U, pressure p and phase fraction α
  • Loads F and M, probes and y⁺ diagnostics
  • Reproducible run and field evidence
06

Baseline boundary

  • No Local3D feedback to Regional2D
  • Rigid geometry; no FSI or damage evolution
  • Observation calibration remains future work
