\section{Future Work}

Near-term project completion work includes observational validation, physical calibration, mesh convergence, timestep convergence, observation-source approval, higher-resolution bathymetry, graded/adaptive mesh selection and publication-quality single-panel figures.

Longer-term framework extensions include HDF5/VTKHDF outputs, CPU/MPI/GPU acceleration, official Kamaishi CAD geometry, porous rubble-mound representation, buildings, FSI, damage/failure modelling and ML optimisation. Porous calibration parameters remain future work until a porous barrier formulation is introduced.
