\section{Conclusions}

The project now has a formal deliverable structure, a shared citation bank and an authoritative consolidated technical report. The G6 baseline closes the theoretical hybrid model: real-event Regional2D forcing is coupled into Local3D OpenFOAM replay, no-defence and rigid-barrier cases reach 300 s, and boundary, wall-function and timestep evidence are accepted for the baseline.

The next scientific step is not further G6 implementation; it is controlled validation and calibration from the frozen baseline commit, after observation approval and numerical-convergence planning.
