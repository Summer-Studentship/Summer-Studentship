\section{Regional2D Governing Model}

Regional propagation is represented by the nonlinear shallow-water equations in conservative variables:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:regional-conserved-state}
\mathbf U=
\begin{bmatrix}
h\\
q_x\\
q_y
\end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}

The balance law is

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:nlswe-balance-law}
\frac{\partial\mathbf U}{\partial t}
+
\frac{\partial\mathbf F}{\partial x}
+
\frac{\partial\mathbf G}{\partial y}
=
\mathbf S .
\end{equation}

The fluxes are

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:nlswe-flux-x}
\mathbf F(\mathbf U)=
\begin{bmatrix}
q_x\\
q_x^2/h+\frac{1}{2}gh^2\\
q_xq_y/h
\end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}

and

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:nlswe-flux-y}
\mathbf G(\mathbf U)=
\begin{bmatrix}
q_y\\
q_xq_y/h\\
q_y^2/h+\frac{1}{2}gh^2
\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

The bed-slope source is

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:bed-slope-source}
\mathbf S_b=
\begin{bmatrix}
0\\
-gh\,\partial z_b/\partial x\\
-gh\,\partial z_b/\partial y
\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

Manning friction is applied as

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:manning-friction-source}
\mathbf S_f=
-
g n^2
\frac{
|\mathbf q|\mathbf q
}{
h^{7/3}
},
\qquad
\mathbf q=
\begin{bmatrix}
q_x\\
q_y
\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

The Coriolis parameter is

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:coriolis-definition}
f=2\Omega\sin\phi .
\end{equation}

Coriolis is treated as a measured physical/model-form input. It is not a blind calibration knob.
